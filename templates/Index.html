Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Standard Upscaling and AI Super-Resolution?

Standard Upscaling uses mathematical algorithms (such as Lanczos or Bicubic) to quickly interpolate and enlarge pixels. AI Super-Resolution uses a deep learning neural network hosted on serverless GPUs to intelligently reconstruct missing textures, crisp linework, and fine visual details without pixelation.

How does AI Super-Resolution handle PNG images with transparency?

Our backend normalizes transparent channels (RGBA) prior to passing processing data to the AI model. For transparent PNG files, background masks and alpha channels are preserved clean so your transparent cutouts remain sharp and usable.

Why does the first AI request take longer to finish?

The AI model runs on Hugging Face's serverless GPU infrastructure. If the inference model is idle when you send a request, it takes 5 to 10 seconds to wake up and load into GPU memory before processing your file.

Are my uploaded images stored or logged on your servers?

No. Every file uploaded for conversion, standard upscaling, enhancement, or AI processing is handled strictly in-memory (RAM) and streamed back to your browser. Garbage collection clears memory buffers immediately after each request.
