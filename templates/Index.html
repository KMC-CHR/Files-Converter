FAQ

What's the difference between Convert, Upscale, and Enhance?

Convert changes format (e.g., HEIC → JPG). Upscale increases resolution (e.g., 1080p → 4K). Enhance improves visual quality (sharpness, contrast, color) without changing size or format.

Are my files safe?

Yes. Files exist in temporary memory during processing and are immediately cleared once sent back to your browser. Nothing is logged or saved.

Does upscaling make my images sharper?

Upscaling uses Lanczos resampling — the sharpest non-AI algorithm. It makes images bigger but cannot add new detail that wasn't in the original. To sharpen existing detail, use the Enhance tab instead.
