Frequently Asked Questions

Can I download MP3 audio files directly from video links?

Yes. Select MP3 (Audio) in the Link Downloader tab to extract audio streams and convert them into MP3 audio files with customizable bitrates (up to 320kbps) powered by FFmpeg.

What video resolutions are available for MP4 downloads?

You can choose maximum resolutions ranging from 360p up to 1080p Full HD. High-definition video and audio tracks are automatically merged on the fly.

What website video links are supported?

Powered by yt-dlp, the downloader supports thousands of sites including YouTube, Vimeo, Twitter/X, TikTok, and direct media link URLs.

What is the file size limit for video downloads?

To preserve server speed and prevent timeouts, single media link downloads are capped at 100MB per request.

How does AI Super-Resolution differ from Standard Upscaling?

Standard Upscaling uses mathematical resampling (Lanczos/Bicubic) to enlarge pixel grids quickly. AI Super-Resolution utilizes deep learning to reconstruct sharp edges, missing line details, and clean transparency masks without blurry pixelation.
